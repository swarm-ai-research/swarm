### Added
- **Attacker-economics model: minimum stake, not maximum monitoring** (`swarm/redteam/attacker_economics.py`, beads `7bwz`) — turns the njvs ladder finding into a governance number. Monitoring at an aggregation level is economically defensible (deters within a false-positive budget) iff the per-unit decomposition cost clears `c_crit = θ_fp·margin·V/M`. Results (run `20260724T023352Z`, M=2, V=100, f_max=0.05): **trajectory-level monitoring is never defensible** (needs per-trajectory cost ~15 vs actual ~0.1, a 150× gap — the economic root of the §6 collapse); **agent-level monitoring is defensible only if the identity stake ≈ 0.52·V** (linear), which is exactly SWARM's `min_stake_to_participate`. The defensible region is a clean diagonal in (stake, objective-value) space. Conclusion (note §7): to defend against objectives worth up to V, set identity stake ≈ 0.52·V; action/trajectory monitors are cost-imposition (force the attacker up to the expensive identity rung), not the catch mechanism — reconnecting the whole DGG→long-horizon→ladder chain to SWARM's existing staking/collusion machinery. Analytic FP validated against simulation (tail-accurate). 10 tests.
- **Adversarial validation: the decomposition ladder** (`swarm/redteam/decomposition_ladder.py`, beads `njvs`) — stress-tests the central claim (aggregate verification beats local) against an ADAPTIVE attacker that knows the active defense and decomposes one rung up, treating "the trajectory monitor breaks" as the hypothesis to confirm. It does. Catch-rate table (run `20260724T021810Z`) is lower-triangular — a defense monitoring up to level m catches an attacker of depth d iff d<m — so **the 0hp7 trajectory monitor's flat ~0.92 collapses to 0.00 against a cross-trajectory attacker** (the monitor is to trajectories what the action filter is to steps), and a cross-agent/Sybil attacker escapes every per-agent defense. Aggregation relocates the blind spot one rung up rather than removing it. The rung that catches the cross-trajectory attacker (per-agent monitor) costs **0.825 false-positive rate** on honest agents — climbing the ladder is not free, its FP cost is explosive. Revised conclusion recorded in note §6 and a caveat added to §2: trajectory monitoring is necessary but not sufficient; the real lever is economic (make decomposition cost exceed the objective's value), not higher monitoring. 9 tests. Corrects the earlier 0hp7 headline.
- **Trajectory monitor: the comparison arm** (`swarm/redteam/circumvention.py` `TrajectoryMonitor*`, beads `0hp7`) — sums per-step suspicion over a trajectory and pauses mid-way when the cumulative crosses a threshold, versus the action-level filter that thresholds each step independently. Head-to-head (run `20260723T170733Z`): where the action filter collapses 0.89→0.00 over horizon 1→8, the monitor holds a **flat ~0.92 catch rate across every horizon** (decomposition- and persistence-proof, since summing is invariant to how finely the attacker splits). Mid-trajectory pause realizes only ~0.52–0.69 of the forbidden mass vs the full mass a post-hoc penalty allows. Honest tradeoff surfaced: the monitor's own false-positive rate accumulates benign activity over long honest trajectories — 0.00 through H=12, rising to 1.00 by H=24 — so at long horizons its threshold/coverage must be tuned, trading catch for fewer interruptions (OpenAI's 'reduce unnecessary interruptions' made quantitative). Not a free win over the action filter, a different operating point. 7 monitor tests (21 total in the suite).
- **Constraint-circumvention trajectory simulation** (`swarm/redteam/circumvention.py`, `experiments/circumvention_horizon_sweep.py`, beads `mtht`) — models the OpenAI long-horizon failure mode (long-horizon-safety-lessons.md §1): a worker spreads a forbidden outcome's suspicion mass `M` over `k` steps at `M/k` each, so an action-level filter (per-step only) can be evaded by a fine-enough decomposition even though cumulative suspicion is invariant at `M`. Standalone trajectory sim rather than a YAML scenario (the orchestrator's atomic `SoftInteraction` can't represent detection-vs-trajectory-length without distortion). Sweep (`20260723T125825Z`, 5 seeds, heterogeneous mass) confirms all three predictions: (1) action-level catch degrades 0.89→0.00 as horizon 1→8 while the evaded attempts' cumulative suspicion rises 0.00→1.01 — coverage collapses exactly where the trajectory signal peaks, the action-channel analog of the plausibility-certificate gap; (2) circumvention success rises 0.11→1.00 with persistence at fixed horizon (manufactured effective horizon); (3) honest completion flat at 1.000 across the grid. Instruction-decay raises circumvention share 0→0.50 with the action filter catching none of it at ample horizon. 14 tests incl. the cumulative-suspicion invariant that the `0hp7` trajectory monitor will exploit.

Unblocks the `mfya` coverage sweep. Also resolves conflict markers committed to this file in cdd1c4de. **Decomposition follow-up:** PCG's composite null (calibrated proxy AND truth-blind acceptance) is now split exactly by cause (`pcg_accepted = pcg_calibration + pcg_selection`) and direction (`pcg_accepted = overtrust − undertrust`); `pcg_overtrust` — mass of accepted certified-bad weighted by proxy belief, identically zero without accepted fabrication under deterministic truth — is surfaced per-epoch as the zero-baselined fabrication indicator. Live split on `escalating_pressure` is total: early PCG −0.386 = calibration −0.386 + selection 0.000, overtrust 0.000 → 0.054 late. The identically-zero selection leg exposed that honest counterparties accept ~everything — acceptance pressure added as a required `mfya` sweep axis.
